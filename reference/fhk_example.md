# A Clean Synthetic Firm Panel for FHK Examples

A synthetic, unbalanced firm panel covering 2010–2012. It contains
stable industry codes, genuine endpoint entry and exit consistent with
registry-style activity windows, raw employment weights, and two types
of pre-computed shares.

## Usage

``` r
fhk_example
```

## Format

A data frame with 20 rows and 10 variables:

- firm_id:

  Synthetic firm identifier.

- year:

  Observation year.

- industry:

  Stable two-sector industry classification.

- productivity:

  Primary synthetic productivity measure.

- productivity_alt:

  Alternative productivity measure.

- employment:

  Raw size weight.

- global_share:

  Employment share summing to one within year.

- within_industry_share:

  Employment share summing to one within industry-year.

- first_active_year:

  Registry-style first activity year.

- last_active_year:

  Registry-style last activity year.

## Source

Generated by `data-raw/generate_example_data.R`.
