# fhktools 0.1.0

* Initial public release.
* Adds strict two-period FHK decomposition with accounting-identity audits.
* Supports raw size weights, within-group shares, and global shares.
* Adds grouped, hierarchical, adjacent-period, and multi-measure workflows.
* Distinguishes sample presence, group membership changes, and registry-validated
  firm entry and exit.
* Includes clean and deliberately complex example panel datasets.
